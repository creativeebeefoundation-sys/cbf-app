## Brand & Style

The design system conveys joyful, humanitarian energy through an approachable, community-first lens. Rooted in optimistic vitality, it replaces solemn institutional charity tropes with radiant warmth, clarity, and delight. 

Targeting passionate donors, grassroots volunteers, partners, and beneficiaries, the visual language merges modern digital craft with tactile cheerfulness:
- **Tone:** Optimistic, empathetic, transparent, and vibrant.
- **Visual Movement:** Soft Organic Modernism with playful pill motifs, airy layouts, and gentle ambient depth.
- **Principles:**
  - *Lightweight Clarity:* Eliminate bureaucratic visual density; prioritize crisp messaging, bite-sized metrics, and breathing room.
  - *Sunny Warmth:* Generous honey-amber highlights balanced against soothing meadow teals.
  - *Approachable Tactility:* Friendly full-pill geometry, soft organic contours, and glowing warm drop shadows that feel touchable and safe.

## Colors

The palette radiates life, balancing sweet honey golden hues with fresh ecosystem mints and zestful coral highlights over warm organic neutrals.

### Roles & Semantic Application
- **Primary (`#D97706` / Honey Sun Amber):** Anchors primary calls-to-action, high-impact donation triggers, and key brand badges. Pair with its bright tint (`#F59E0B`) for hover states and energetic accents.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` / Meadow Teal):** Represents sustainability, trust, and community vitality. Utilized for impact narratives, success indicators, and secondary supportive actions. Light mint variations (`#14B8A6`, `#CCFBF1`) serve as container fills.
- **Tertiary (`#F97316` / Warm Tangerine Coral):** Sparks urgency and delight. Applied to alert banners, playful celebration tags, matching-gift tags, and live campaign counters.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAFAF9` (Warm Stone/Cream) provides a soft, glare-free ground.
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pristine Milk) creates radiant contrast against the cream canvas.
  - `text-primary`: `#1C1917` (Deep Warm Charcoal) ensures AA/AAA legibility without the harshness of pure black.
  - `text-muted`: `#78716C` (Warm Stone Grey) maintains supportive hierarchy for meta info and helper notes.

## Typography

The pairing between **Outfit** and **Plus Jakarta Sans** strikes an ideal equilibrium between geometric cheer and effortless readability.

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit):** Generously proportioned with circular letterforms that feel optimistic, modern, and open-hearted. Large headings utilize tighter letter spacing to maintain graphic cohesion.
- **Body & Functional Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Highly legible humanist sans-serif with subtle warmth. The wide apertures and clean terminals preserve readability across micro-copy, donation forms, and long-form narrative case studies.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Keep paragraph lengths short (maximum 60–65 characters per line) to maintain an airy, digestible presentation. Emphasize key statistics using `headline-xl` alongside cheerful colored labels.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column grid system drives desktop layouts, condensing dynamically into an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile.

- **Spacing Rhythm:** Structured around a baseline 4px/8px rhythm. Generous vertical breathing room is enforced to avoid visual fatigue and bureaucratic density.
- **Section Pacing:** Alternate content blocks with open canvas space. Key modules (donation calculators, hero banners, visual testimonials) require generous padding (`space-xl` or greater) to feel elevated and inviting.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile (< 640px)`: 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (20px), single-column card flows.
  - `Tablet (640px - 1024px)`: 8 columns, `margin` (32px), 2-column card structures.
  - `Desktop (> 1024px)`: 12 columns, max content container of `1280px`, fluid gutters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop shadows and stark borders in favor of warm, glowing, ambient tiers:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral warm canvas (`#FAFAF9`).
- **Level 1 (Card Resting):** Surface milk (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm tinted ambient shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03);`
  Complemented by an ultra-subtle border: `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Floating Badges):** Slightly lifted state for cards and active donation amounts:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.06);`
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Prominent warm drop shadow with gentle backdrop filter:
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.14); backdrop-filter: blur(8px);`

## Shapes

The design system embraces high roundedness (Level 3 / Pill-shaped) to reinforce its welcoming, human-first character.

- **Buttons & Chips:** Pure pill styling (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) across all sizes.
- **Cards & Impact Modules:** Large corner radii (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) to create smooth, soft containers that feel approachable and gentle.
- **Images & Avatars:** Rounded capsules, soft-edged squircles, or circular portraits with cheerful pastel stroke accents (`3px solid #FEF3C7` or `#CCFBF1`).
- **Form Inputs:** Soft pill or rounded-xl outlines (`1rem` to `9999px`) to maintain tactile harmony with interactive buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Full pill shape, filled with Sun Amber gradient (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`), bold white text (`Outfit`, 600), and a soft amber glow shadow. Scale up slightly (`1.02x`) on hover.
- **Secondary Button:** Full pill shape, mint tint background (`#CCFBF1`), deep teal text (`#0D9488`), and zero border. Turns slightly deeper mint on hover.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** Transparent fill, 2px border in `#FDE68A` or `#99F6E4`, matching text color.

### Chips & Badges
- High-contrast, micro-pill indicators.
- Pastel tinted surfaces (`#FEF3C7` for Amber, `#CCFBF1` for Teal, `#FFEDD5` for Coral) combined with darkened, high-contrast text (`#B45309`, `#0F766E`, `#C2410C`).
- Ideal for project tags (e.g., "🐝 Wildlife", "🌱 Reforestation", "💧 Clean Water").

### Impact & Donation Cards
- Milk-white canvas with 24px (`1.5rem`) corner radius and Level 1 warm shadow.
- Feature quick donation preset selectors formatted as rounded pill toggles.
- Include lively progress bars using amber-to-teal gradients set inside soft warm-cream troughs.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Generously spaced item rows separated by generous whitespace rather than stark horizontal dividers.
- Visual bullet points replaced with cheerful colored micro-pills or custom circular iconography.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** Pill-shaped text fields on `#FFFFFF` with warm stone placeholder text. Focus state brings a 2px stroke in `#D97706` and a soft 4px amber halo (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Smooth rounded squares and circles featuring custom check/dot glyphs filled with Teal (`#0D9488`) when checked.

### Cause Metric Highlights (Specialty Component)
- Distinct circular or pill-shaped counter cards containing oversized Outfit numerals (`headline-xl`), paired with cheerful descriptive pill labels beneath to clearly communicate impact milestones.
